Sanwa Supply, an Okayama-based maker of PC accessories, has put a small body camera on sale in Japan. The 400-CAMBD002 clips to a shirt or uniform and records hands-free. It went on sale on 19 November through the company's Sanwa Direct shop.

Kaden Watch frames it as a tool against abusive customers, a growing topic in Japan. The press release lists patrols, equipment checks and recording service encounters under company rules.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | About 50g |
| Size | About 38 × 21 × 55mm (W × D × H) |
| Lens | 133-degree wide angle |
| Video | Up to 2,304 × 1,296 |
| Battery | 1,200mAh, up to about 6 hours of continuous recording |
| Screen | 0.96-inch LCD on top |
| Storage | microSD card (card not mentioned as included in our sources) |
| Protection | IP64 per the press release and Kaden Watch |
| Mounting | Clip or magnet attachment |
| Price | 16,800 yen including tax at time of writing (15,273 yen before tax) |

## Why it is interesting

Most body cameras are sold for police and security firms, with cloud software and a monthly fee. This one is a plain recorder. It saves to a microSD card and does no wireless communication at all, according to Sanwa. Footage stays on the card until someone copies it off.

It also has loop recording. When the card fills, the camera overwrites the oldest files. You can take a still photo while a video is recording.

The 15,273-yen figure is the pre-tax price in Sanwa's press release. INTERNET Watch calls the same number the expected market price. Kaden Watch gives 16,800 yen as the direct price.

One detail to watch: the product title on the au Pay Market listing says IP67. The press release and both news articles say IP64. We go with IP64, which is what the maker's own release states.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We found no sign of an overseas sale. It looks like a Japan-only product.**

- **Where:** It is sold through Sanwa Direct and the company's stores on Rakuten, Yahoo Shopping, au Pay Market and Amazon.co.jp. We could not confirm that any of them ship this item abroad. A Japanese proxy buying service such as Buyee or ZenMarket is the likely route, but we did not check that they accept it.
- **The catch:** The product has no wireless features and no app, so there is no cloud or language lock. The listing mentions a Japanese-language manual and a one-year warranty, which we assume applies only in Japan. We could not confirm whether the menus have an English option.
- **Radio and safety marks:** We could not confirm any FCC or CE filing. Because the camera has no radio, the rules are lighter, but the lithium battery may still matter for shipping. We have not checked that.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm one.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Body-worn cameras from Motorola Solutions, Axon and many smaller brands are widely sold. Prices and features vary a lot. Check local law on recording people, since the rules differ by country and by workplace.
