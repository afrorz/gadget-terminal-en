KDDI, the Japanese carrier behind the au brand, has announced the INFOBAR ZERO. It is a new phone in the INFOBAR line, which began in 2003. It does calls and SMS. It does not browse the web, and it has no social media or chat apps. KDDI announced it on November 18 and will sell it from December 25.

The last INFOBAR was the INFOBAR xv in 2018. ITmedia says this is a "dumbphone" aimed at people who want to use their smartphone less, with young people as the main target. KDDI expects sales of about one-tenth of earlier INFOBAR models, according to Keitai Watch.

The chip, talk time and LTE band list come from Smhn's report of the launch event, not from the KDDI release. Smhn lists an MT8765WA chip, about 360 minutes of VoLTE talk time and about 240 hours of standby.

## Why it is interesting

The keys are acrylic tiles. Keitai Watch and ITmedia both say this is the first return of acrylic keys since the original INFOBAR 23 years ago. Product designer Naoto Fukasawa has worked on the line since 2001, according to Keitai Watch.

The phone does a few things on purpose. It has a phone book, calendar, alarm and calculator. It plays music you copy from a PC or smartphone, with no streaming. Wired earphones plug into the USB-C port. The camera is about 2 megapixels, chosen for a retro look.

Keitai Watch reports that the pitch is a small set of things: contacts for about 10 close people, or about 20 albums. It also works with au's second-number service, which costs 550 yen per month at time of writing, so a smartphone user can move a second number onto it.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Not in any way we could confirm. It is a Japan-only product with no overseas plan.**

- **Where:** KDDI lists au Online Shop, au stores, Okinawa Cellular stores, Amazon.co.jp and several electronics chains such as Yodobashi Camera and Bic Camera. All are Japanese channels. We could not confirm that any of them ships this phone abroad. A Japanese address or a forwarding service is the likely route.
- **The catch:** Keitai Watch says overseas use was not a design focus and no operation is guaranteed. Smhn lists LTE bands 1, 2, 3, 8, 18, 19, 26 and 41. Its 3G bands are listed as unusable in Japan, and the phone is not meant for 3G. Check these bands against your carrier before you buy. The phone supports Japanese and English, per Smhn. The plans KDDI names are au's Keitai plan, second number and povo2.0. We could not confirm that a foreign SIM works with calls and SMS.
- **Global version:** We found none, and no announcement of one. We found no FCC or CE listing.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Simple phones from other makers exist in most markets. We did not compare them with the INFOBAR ZERO, so we do not name one here.
