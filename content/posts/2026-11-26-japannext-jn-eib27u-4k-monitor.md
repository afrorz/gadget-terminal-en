JAPANNEXT is a monitor brand headquartered in Isumi, Chiba. On 26 November 2026 it put the JN-EiB27U on sale. It is a 27-inch 4K IPS monitor limited to e-commerce sites, at 32,980 yen, at time of writing. It launched alongside two other models, as reported by PC Watch.

Note that PC Watch gives a different model, the JN-IPSB27U, at 38,980 yen. A second JAPANNEXT press release lists the 38,980 yen price for that model.

## Why it is interesting

This is not a flashy monitor. It has no USB-C input and runs at 60Hz. What stands out is the price for the color coverage: a 4K IPS panel claiming 97% DCI-P3 and 90% Adobe RGB for around 33,000 yen. Figures are JAPANNEXT's own, and we have not seen a measured review.

JAPANNEXT sells mostly through Amazon.co.jp, Rakuten and Yahoo Shopping in Japan, which is why Japanese tech sites cover its launches closely.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Not directly. The brand sells in Europe, but we could not confirm this model there.**

- **Where:** The model is on JAPANNEXT's Japanese shop, Amazon.co.jp, Rakuten and Yahoo Shopping. Amazon.co.jp lists a filter for international shipping eligibility, but large monitors may not qualify. We could not confirm this model's eligibility.
- **Global version:** JAPANNEXT runs a French store, japannext.fr, with an English version. It lists other 4K 27-inch models, for example a white USB-C model at 249 euros, at time of writing. We could not confirm that the JN-EiB27U is sold there.
- **The catch:** We could not confirm the power adapter, plug type or CE or FCC marking for this model. A monitor bought from Japan may come with a Japanese 100V plug.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Many 27-inch 4K IPS monitors from Dell, LG, Gigabyte and AOC fill the same slot. Check each one for USB-C input, which this model lacks.
