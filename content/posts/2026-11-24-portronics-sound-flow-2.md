Portronics is a New Delhi company that sells phone accessories and small audio gear. On 24 November 2026 it launched the Sound Flow 2. It is a "4-in-1" device: a Bluetooth speaker, a wireless charger, a digital alarm clock and a white-noise player. It is meant for a bedside table or a desk.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Speaker output | 16W |
| Bluetooth | 5.3, with TWS mode to pair two speakers |
| Wireless charging | Up to 15W, pad on top |
| Power adapter | Minimum 18W needed for best charging (not stated as included) |
| Extras | Digital clock display, alarm, white noise, 360° LED lighting |
| Design | Cylindrical, black, fabric-mesh body |
| Product code | POR 3667 |
| Warranty | 1 year |
| MRP | 3,499 rupees |
| Special price (at time of writing) | 2,099 rupees on the Portronics store |

## Why it is interesting

Bedside gadgets usually need three plugs: a speaker, a charging pad and a clock. This one combines them. The white-noise function and the clock make it a sleep aid as much as a speaker. The pad on top charges a compatible phone, and the front display shows the time.

The price is the other point. The special price is 2,099 rupees, well below the 3,499 rupee MRP. Portronics lists the specs above. Fone Arena repeats them and adds the retail channels. We did not find an independent test, so the sound quality and charging speed are unverified.

One gap: neither source says what is in the box. The Portronics page says a power adapter of at least 18W is needed for best charging, and Fone Arena says the same. We could not confirm whether an adapter is included.

## Can you get it outside India?

**We could not confirm any sale outside India.**

- **Where:** In India it is sold on the Portronics online store, Amazon.in, Flipkart and in other online and offline shops, according to Fone Arena. We did not find a Portronics store for other countries, and we did not check whether Amazon.in or Flipkart ship this item abroad.
- **The catch:** The speaker needs a mains adapter, and we could not confirm what plug it uses or whether one is supplied. A charger rated for your local mains and at least 18W should work, but that is our reading of the Portronics note, not a maker statement. There is no app mentioned. We could not confirm FCC or CE marks, or whether the one-year warranty applies outside India.
- **Global version:** We found no overseas launch announcement.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Speakers with a built-in wireless charging pad and clock are sold by several brands in many markets. We did not compare them with this model.
