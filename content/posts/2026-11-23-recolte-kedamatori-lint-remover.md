Winners, a Tokyo appliance company, sells small kitchen and home goods under the recolte name. On 13 November 2026 it launched Kedamatori, a cordless lint remover. The name means "pill remover". It has a handle-type body.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Blades | Six inner blades with a wide outer blade |
| Speeds | Low, medium, high |
| Battery | Lithium-ion, 1,800mAh |
| Charging | USB-C, DC 5V 1.0A, about 3 hours |
| Runtime | About 2.5 hours |
| Display | Shows battery level and speed |
| Dust box | Magnetic, removable |
| Size | About 6.7 × 7.9 × 18.2cm (W × D × H) |
| Weight | About 260g |
| Colors | Cream White, Ash Green |
| In the box | Body, cleaning brush, USB-C cable, spare inner blade, manual |
| List price (at time of writing) | 3,850 yen with tax |

## Why it is interesting

Lint removers are cheap and common. This one is aimed at people who worry about damaging knitwear. Winners says the six inner blades and the wide outer blade work together to remove pills while protecting the cloth. A fan pulls the lint into the dust box.

The handle shape is meant for larger jobs. The release mentions several garments in a row and fabric sofas. The runtime of about 2.5 hours is long for this type of tool. These are the maker's figures. We have not seen an independent test.

The care parts are simple. The dust box detaches by magnet. The inner and outer blades come off for cleaning, and the brush stores in the base. Kaden Watch reports the same figures as the press release.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any overseas sale. Treat it as Japan-only for now.**

- **Where:** The press release gives no store list. We did not find a recolte shop that ships abroad. Japanese retailers and proxy shopping services are the likely route, but we did not check whether either accepts this item.
- **The catch:** It charges over USB-C at 5V, so there is no voltage or plug problem. There is no app. The manual and any warranty are in Japanese. We could not confirm whether the warranty applies abroad, or whether it has FCC or CE marks.
- **Global version:** We found no English product page and no announcement of an overseas launch.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Rechargeable fabric shavers are sold by many brands in most markets. We did not compare them with this model.
