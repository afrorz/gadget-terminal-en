Tescom has launched the Facial Point Care Skin Tap, model HCB-STT01. It is a small handheld device that vibrates against the face and neck. Kaden Watch reports it went on sale in mid-November in Japan. Tescom sets no list price. The direct price is 11,980 yen at time of writing, per Kaden Watch.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Size | About 90 × 36 × 65mm (W × D × H) |
| Weight | About 200g |
| Tips | One silicone pointer and one glass pointer |
| Modes | Line care mode (three levels: 1,600, 2,000 and 2,400rpm) and tapping mode (800rpm) |
| Power | Built-in lithium-ion battery, charged over USB Type-C at DC 5V/2A |
| Charging time | About 2.5 hours |
| Runtime | About 2 hours in tapping mode |
| Timer | Switches off by itself after 5 minutes |
| In the box | Quick manual, both pointers, USB charging cable, storage pouch (no AC adapter) |
| Warranty | One year |

## Why it is interesting

Skin-care gadgets with vibration are common. This one is built around a two-step routine. Step one uses the soft silicone tip on tense spots. Step two uses the round glass tip, which Tescom says glides over skin with little friction, along the jawline.

Tescom says you do not need a special gel. You can use it after your normal skin care or away from home. The company's manual advises a light touch, not hard pressure, and suggests trying the vibration on your palm first.

Tescom's page also cites a test of 22 women in each of two age groups, 30s and 40s, run by a company called SOUKEN. The page notes that results vary by person and that the changes are temporary. We did not see the test details, so we do not repeat the results here.

The charging side is easy for travellers. It uses a standard USB-C port, so there is no Japanese-only plug or voltage issue on the device. The adapter is not included. Tescom says to use one rated 5V/2A (10W).

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any overseas sale. Treat it as a Japan-only product for now.**

- **Where:** Tescom's page links to its official stores on Rakuten and Yahoo Shopping Japan. We could not confirm that either ships abroad. A Japanese proxy shopping service is the likely route, but we did not check that this item is accepted.
- **The catch:** The manual and warranty are for Japan. We could not confirm whether the warranty applies abroad. There is no app, so no cloud or language lock.
- **Global version:** Tescom's site has a "Global" link. We did not find this model on an English page, and we could not confirm a global version.
- **Safety marks:** We could not confirm any FCC or CE filing. The device has a lithium-ion battery, which may matter for international shipping.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Handheld facial massagers and gua sha style tools are sold by many brands in most markets. We have not compared them with this model.
