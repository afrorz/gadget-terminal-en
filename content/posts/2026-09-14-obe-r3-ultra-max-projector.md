OBE (大眼橙) is a Chinese projector brand that is little known outside China. Its new R3 Ultra Max has a Z-shaped gimbal built into the body. It tilts 120 degrees up and down and turns 360 degrees, so it can project onto the ceiling. The 2.1-channel speaker in the base keeps facing forward while the projector moves.

It sells on JD.com for 3,999 yuan (at time of writing). IT之家 reports it can drop to 2,799 yuan in some Chinese regions with government subsidies.

## What it does differently

Gimbal projectors usually trade sound for size. OBE went the other way and built a real subwoofer into the base, with a 1,070cc sound chamber. It also does auto keystone, auto focus and obstacle avoidance with a ToF sensor and a camera. The software is OBE's own OS with AirPlay and Miracast, plus voice search powered by Tencent's Hunyuan AI model.

## Can you get it outside China?

**Not easily.** At time of writing it is sold only on JD.com, and we found no announcement of sales outside China.

- **How:** A buying agent is the realistic route. We could not confirm whether JD.com ships this model abroad. At 5.22kg, shipping will not be cheap.
- **The catch:** The OS and voice search are built for China. We could not confirm an English interface. If you import it, plan to use it mainly as an HDMI projector.
- **Certification:** We could not confirm FCC or CE certification.
- **Alternative you can buy locally:** XGIMI sells gimbal projectors with ceiling projection in many countries, with local support.
