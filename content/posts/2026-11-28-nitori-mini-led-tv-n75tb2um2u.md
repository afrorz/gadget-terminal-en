Nitori, the Sapporo-based furniture chain, has sold its own TVs for some time. In late November 2026 it added a 75-inch size to its 4K Mini LED line, next to the existing 55-inch and 65-inch models. Nitori says they are sold at its stores nationwide and on Nitori's web shop.

## Why it is interesting

Nitori says a new panel layer, which it calls the "all-corner clear panel", raises brightness by about 10 percent over its previous 55-inch and 65-inch models. It also says brightness is more even toward the edges. Those are Nitori's own figures. We have not seen an independent measurement, and Nitori does not state a peak brightness.

The audio modes were retuned for movies, music and news. Again, no independent test.

The unusual detail is the tuner. The set has two terrestrial and two BS/110-degree CS digital tuners, but Nitori states that it cannot receive 4K or 8K satellite broadcasts. That is a Japan-specific limit. Outside Japan the tuners do nothing, so the TV would work only as a streaming display.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Not in any practical way. It is built for Japanese broadcasting and 100V mains.**

- **Where:** Nitori stores in Japan and nitori-net.jp. We could not confirm whether the web shop ships TVs abroad. TVs fall under Japan's appliance recycling rules, and the page lists a collection fee for replacement purchases.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm any model sold outside Japan.
- **The catch:** The set is rated AC 100V. In 220 to 240V countries you would need a heavy step-down transformer for 250W. The tuners support only Japanese broadcast standards. Google TV and Google Cast apps may work abroad, but we could not confirm regional app support. We could not confirm CE, UKCA or FCC marking.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Mini LED TVs in 55 to 75 inches are widely sold by TCL, Hisense and Samsung, with the right voltage and tuners for your region.
