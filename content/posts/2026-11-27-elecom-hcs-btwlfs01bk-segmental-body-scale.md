Elecom announced the HCS-BTWLFS01BK on 26 November 2026. It goes on sale in late November in Japan. Elecom says it is the company's first scale to measure body composition in five segments. The price is open. Elecom's direct shop lists 20,980 yen, at time of writing, per Kaden Watch.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Measurement | Whole body plus torso, right arm, left arm, right leg, left leg |
| Modes | General mode and "muscular" mode |
| Connection | Wi-Fi, sends to the ELECOM Healthcare app (iOS and Android) |
| Display | Colour TFT, 19 items on the scale |
| App items | Up to 34 |
| Users | 5 |
| Maximum load | 150kg |
| Size | About 300 x 330 x 35mm |
| Weight | About 2.2kg |
| Power | Step-on, no switch |
| Price | 20,980 yen at the Elecom direct shop, at time of writing |

## Why it is interesting

Segmental readings matter to people who train one side or one limb and want to see the balance. The scale sends results by Wi-Fi when you step on it. You do not need to open the phone app each time.

The muscular mode is for people with high muscle mass. Elecom says the normal mode can give odd readings for them because of differences in body water and muscle distribution. It says the muscular mode changes the calculation. The estimation formula comes from a research project supervised by Dr. Kiyoji Tanaka, per Elecom. We have not seen an independent accuracy test.
