GPD, the Shenzhen maker of small Windows handhelds, has a new WIN Mini. It is called the WIN Mini Ultra. It uses Intel's Arc G3 Extreme chip instead of the AMD chips in earlier WIN Mini models.

GPD has now published a full spec sheet on its global site. The figures below come from that page unless we say otherwise. Early reports disagreed on the screen, battery and memory. GPD's page settles all three. There is still no price.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Chip | Intel Arc G3 Extreme, 14 cores (2P+8E+4LPE), up to 4.7 GHz |
| Graphics | Intel Arc B390, 12 Xe3 cores |
| Screen | 8-inch 1920×1200 LCD, 48–120Hz variable refresh, touch |
| Brightness | 500 nits standard, 575 nits maximum |
| Memory | 32GB or 64GB LPDDR5X |
| Storage | One M.2 2280 slot, PCIe 5.0, 512GB to 4TB |
| Battery | 56.01Wh, 100W USB-C charger in the box |
| Ports | 2× USB4 v2 (80Gbps), 1× USB-A 3.2 Gen 2, microSD Express |
| Input | Backlit keyboard, touchpad, capacitive sticks, Hall-effect triggers |
| Size and weight | 182.5 × 133 × 30mm, 845g |
| Price | Not announced |

GPD's own 3DMark test, as reported by TweakTown, gave a Time Spy score of 7,399. The same test gave 4,180 for the Core Ultra 7 258V. These are company figures. We found no independent tests.

## Why it is interesting

The 12 Xe3 graphics cores are the headline. If GPD's numbers hold up, this would be a large step up in graphics over the earlier WIN Mini.

It is also much heavier. At 845g it is far from the 555g of the 2025 WIN Mini, as listed by TweakTown. That makes it a different kind of device from the pocketable original.

The two USB4 v2 ports are unusual. GPD says they can drive its G1 and G2 external graphics docks.

## Can you get it outside China?

**GPD plans to sell it worldwide, but it is not on sale yet.**

- **Where:** GPD lists the WIN Mini Ultra on its global site, gpd.hk, with an "Order Now" button. At time of writing the button did not lead to a store page. GPD sells through distributors outside China. We could not confirm which of them will carry this model.
- **The catch:** There is no price. A search summary says GPD may run a crowdfunding campaign first. We could not confirm this. PC Games Hardware expects it to cost less than the 2025 WIN Mini with the Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, which it says starts at about 1,850 euros at time of writing. That is a guess by the outlet, not a GPD price.
- **Global version:** GPD's spec sheet lists a global warranty of one year. It also lists charger plugs for China, the US, the UK, the EU and Japan. Windows 11 Home comes pre-installed. We found no China-only apps or services in the spec sheet.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** The 2025 WIN Mini with the Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 is sold now. We did not check other handhelds against the Ultra's graphics claims.
