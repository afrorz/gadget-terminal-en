GPD, the Shenzhen maker of small Windows handhelds, has confirmed a new WIN Mini. It is called the WIN Mini Ultra. It uses Intel's Arc G3 Extreme chip instead of the AMD chips in earlier WIN Mini models.

This is an early look. GPD has not announced a price or a release date. We could not read GPD's own product page, and the TweakTown and TechPowerUp pages returned 403. The details below come from PC Games Hardware, which we read, and from search and digest summaries of the other two. The reports disagree in places, and we say where.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Chip | Intel Arc G3 Extreme, 14 cores (2P+8E+4LPE), up to 4.7 GHz per TechPowerUp |
| Graphics | Arc B390, 12 Xe3 cores |
| Screen | 8-inch 1920×1200 IPS, 48–120Hz per TechPowerUp. PC Games Hardware lists 9.8 inches |
| Brightness | 575 nits per PC Games Hardware |
| Battery | 56.01Wh per TweakTown summary, 54Wh per PC Games Hardware |
| Memory | Up to 64GB LPDDR5X per TweakTown summary. PC Games Hardware lists 32GB |
| Ports | 2× USB4 v2 (80Gbps per TweakTown summary), plus USB-A and microSD per PC Games Hardware |
| Design | Clamshell with keyboard and touchpad, Hall-effect triggers |
| Price | Not announced |

PC Games Hardware says its figures come from a leaked datasheet, not from GPD. The TweakTown summary says one GPD graphic labels the screen 9.8 inches while its comparison chart shows 8 inches. Treat screen size, battery and memory as unsettled.

GPD's own 3DMark test, as reported by TweakTown, gave a Time Spy score of 7,399. The same test gave 4,180 for the Core Ultra 7 258V. These are company figures. We found no independent tests.

## Why it is interesting

The 12 Xe3 graphics cores are the headline. If GPD's numbers hold up, this would be a large step up in graphics over the earlier WIN Mini.

It also looks bigger. A search summary of the TweakTown report lists a weight of about 845g, against 555g for the 2025 WIN Mini. That makes it a different kind of device from the pocketable original.

## Can you get it outside China?

**Probably, but we could not confirm anything about this model.**

- **Where:** No sale has started. We could not confirm where GPD will sell it, or whether it will ship outside China. PC Games Hardware quotes euro prices for the 2025 model from the manufacturer, which suggests GPD sells to Europe.
- **The catch:** A search summary says GPD is expected to run a crowdfunding campaign. We could not confirm this. PC Games Hardware expects it to cost less than the 2025 WIN Mini with the Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, which it says starts at about 1,850 euros at time of writing. That is a guess by the outlet, not a GPD price.
- **Global version:** GPD has not announced a separate version. We could not confirm any region lock.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** The 2025 WIN Mini with the Ryzen AI 9 HX 370 is listed by GPD at the price above. We did not check other handhelds against the Ultra's graphics claims.
