CCP, a Tokyo appliance maker, runs a character-goods brand called Charanics. It has made a heated plush from Tamagotchi, the Bandai virtual pet that turns 30. There are two characters: Mametchi and Mimitchi. Each has a USB heater in its belly. You plug it in, and it warms up.

Kaden Watch says pre-orders opened on November 18 at the CCP online store, with sales starting in late November. CCP's press release says it will also go through online shops and goods stores.

## Specs

| | Mametchi | Mimitchi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | KS-AN66-TMA | KS-AN66-TMI |
| Length | About 300mm | About 315mm |
| Power | USB Type-A, DC 5V, 2A, 4.5W | Same |
| Cable | About 1.8m | Same |
| Warm-up | Belly reaches about 42 to 46 degrees C in about 30 seconds | Same |
| Auto shut-off | After about 60 minutes | Same |
| Materials | Polyester plush, PVC cable | Same |
| Price | 7,260 yen including tax, at time of writing | Same |

CCP says it works with a power bank.

## Why it is interesting

It is a plush with a heater, so it is closer to a lap warmer than a toy. The idea is to hold it or put it on your knees. The USB power also means a desk or a train seat with a power bank would do.

The brand is the other half of the story. Charanics sells character appliances in Japan, and the licence line reads (C)BANDAI. We found no sign that the range is sold outside Japan.

We did not find independent tests of the heater. The warm-up figure is CCP's own. The press release lists no safety information, and we could not confirm any.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any way to buy it outside Japan, apart from a forwarding service.**

- **Where:** The CCP online store is the main shop at launch. We could not confirm that it ships abroad. A Japanese proxy or forwarding service is the likely route, but we have not tested one.
- **The catch:** The plug is USB Type-A at 5V, so voltage and plug type are not a problem. A heater like this may still face rules in your country. We did not look into that. A phone charger or power bank with an A port is enough to run it.
- **Global version:** We found none, and no announcement of one. We could not confirm that Bandai plans one.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** USB-heated plush toys and microwavable plush warmers are sold in many countries. We did not test them, so we name none here.
