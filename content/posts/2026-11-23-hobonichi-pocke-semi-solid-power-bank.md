Hobonichi is best known for its Techo planners. On 1 December 2026 it will start selling a gadget instead: POCKé, a pocket-shaped mobile battery. It goes on sale at 11:00 JST in the Hobonichi online store, at TOBICHI Tokyo and at the Hobonichi Techo Store in Tokyo Station.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Battery | Semi-solid-state lithium-ion, 3.6V 5000mAh (18Wh) |
| Wireless output | Qi2, up to 15W (5W, 7.5W, 10W, 15W steps) |
| Wired output | USB-C, 5V/3000mAh, 9V/1650mAh, 12V/1250mAh |
| Input | USB-C, 5V/3A or 9V/1.8A |
| Charge time | About 130 minutes from empty with a PD 18W charger |
| Charge cycles | About 2,000, depending on use |
| Operating temperature | -10 to 45°C when discharging, 0 to 35°C when charging |
| Size and weight | About 98 × 17 × 158mm, about 165g (body) |
| In the box | Body, carabiner, quick start guide with warranty, cover swap parts, 30cm USB-C cable |
| Price (at time of writing) | 9,900 yen with cover, 7,260 yen body only, 2,640 yen for a spare cover, all with tax |

## Why it is interesting

Most power banks look like gadgets. POCKé looks like a small fabric pocket. You slide the phone in, and the magnet in the back holds it against the Qi2 pad. There is no cable. Hobonichi sells eight cover designs at launch, including quilted, checked and printed patterns, and says it will add more.

The cell is a semi-solid type, with a jelly-like electrolyte. Hobonichi says this lowers the risk of leaks and fire compared with a normal liquid cell, and that the battery wears out more slowly. The company claims a life about four times longer. We could not check that figure independently. It is a company claim.

The press release says it can be carried on a plane, but tells buyers to check each airline's rules first. Hobonichi also notes it is a member of JBRC, Japan's small rechargeable battery recycling group. That only helps if you live in Japan.

The release lists iPhone 12 to iPhone 17 and iPhone Air as supported. Other MagSafe and Qi2 phones may work. Hobonichi says it does not guarantee every device, and that a non-MagSafe case will not hold the phone.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Possibly, but Hobonichi itself says overseas use is not covered by the warranty.**

- **Where:** The sale is announced for the Hobonichi online store and two Tokyo shops. Hobonichi's English help pages say its store ships to many countries, and that some products cannot be shipped overseas. We could not confirm whether POCKé is shipped abroad. A Japanese proxy shopping service is another route, but we did not check one.
- **The catch:** The release says problems caused by use overseas are not covered by the warranty. The 5V and 9V USB-C input works with common chargers. We could not confirm FCC or CE marks, and we found no regulatory filing for the Qi2 transmitter.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm any English page for POCKé or any overseas launch plan.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Magnetic Qi2 power banks are sold by many brands in most markets. We did not compare them. None that we know of use a swappable fabric cover.
