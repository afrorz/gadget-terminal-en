Nitori is a Sapporo-based furniture chain that now sells its own electronics. Toffy is a retro-styled kitchen appliance brand from the Tokyo company Radonna. In mid-November 2026 the two released three small appliances in a muted green called Smoky Leaf. Nitori says the colour is sold only at Nitori. They went on sale at some Nitori stores and on Nitori's web shop, according to both companies' releases.

| Notes | Folds one slice of bread into a half-size sandwich. Removable, washable plates | One-cup drip. Water tank 480ml | Fits one slice. Top and bottom heaters, 15-minute timer |

All three are rated AC 100V, 50/60Hz. Prices include tax.

## Why it is interesting

These are small, single-person appliances, which is a common size in Japan. The sandwich maker folds one slice of bread, so the result is a half-size sandwich. Its cord winds around the back, and it can stand on end for storage.

The coffee maker has a height-adjustable tray. Nitori says it works with tumblers up to about 320ml and with shallow mugs. It has three brew modes. MILD is continuous. MEDIUM and STRONG are intermittent, with a steaming pause. Nitori describes the taste of each mode. We have not seen an independent test.

The toaster interior is about 160 x 150 x 65mm. Nitori notes that some loaves, such as a domed loaf, may not fit even as a single slice.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Not through Nitori, as far as we could confirm. Toffy's regular colours are a different matter, but we did not check them.**

- **Where:** Some Nitori stores in Japan and Nitori's web shop, nitori-net.jp. We could not confirm whether the web shop ships these items abroad.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm a version of the Smoky Leaf models sold outside Japan.
- **The catch:** All three are 100V products. In 220 to 240V countries they need a step-down transformer, and a heating appliance draws a lot through one. We could not confirm CE, UKCA or FCC marking, and plug type is not stated in our sources.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Single-slice toasters, one-cup drip coffee makers and sandwich presses are widely sold in Europe and North America at the right voltage. Buy one made for your mains.
