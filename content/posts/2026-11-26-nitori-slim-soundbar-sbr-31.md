Nitori is a Sapporo-based furniture and home-goods chain with stores across Japan. This autumn it started an audio line called life+audio. The newest product is the SBR-31, a slim soundbar with a wireless subwoofer. It goes on sale in early December 2026 at some Nitori stores and on Nitori's online shop.

The pitch is that the bar sits in front of a TV without blocking the screen and without more cables. The bar is about 4cm tall.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Channels | 2.1, with Dolby Atmos support |
| Amplifier output | 120W total (bar 30W x 2, subwoofer 60W) |
| Drivers | Bar: 3.8cm tweeter and 3.0 x 10.5cm mid driver; subwoofer: 13cm |
| Frequency range | Bar 140Hz to 20kHz; subwoofer 50Hz to 140Hz |
| Inputs | HDMI (eARC/ARC), Bluetooth 5.3, USB (MP3 playback) |
| Sound modes | Voice, Movie, Music, Game |
| Bar size | About 80.0cm wide, 3.45cm deep, 4.0cm high; 0.71kg |
| Subwoofer size | About 10.5cm wide, 35.0cm deep, 25.3cm high; 3.34kg; stands upright or lies flat |
| In the box | Remote, HDMI cable, AC adapter, power cable, wall-mount kit |
| Colors | Dark gray, beige |
| Price | 29,900 yen, at time of writing |

## Why it is interesting

Soundbars at this price usually come from audio brands. This one comes from a furniture store, and it is sold next to sofas and TV stands. Nitori built the design around a living room: a beige option, a subwoofer that links on its own when powered, and a single HDMI cable to the TV.

Dolby Atmos support on a 2.1-channel bar means it decodes the format. The press release says you need Atmos content and a compatible source. It does not claim height speakers, and we would not expect real overhead sound from a bar this thin. We have not seen independent measurements or reviews, since the product is not yet on sale.

HDMI-CEC and ARC/eARC need to be enabled on the TV for the one-cable control to work, according to Nitori's notes.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any sale outside Japan, so treat it as Japan-only for now.**

- **Where:** Nitori sells it at some Japanese stores and on its Japanese online shop. We found no international shipping policy for that shop. A tourist guide we found says Nitori does not offer international shipping for overseas visitors. Nitori also runs stores in Taiwan, mainland China and other Asian markets, but we could not confirm that the SBR-31 or the life+audio line is sold there.
- **The catch:** The bundled AC adapter is a Japanese-market item. We could not confirm its voltage range or plug type, so check before using it abroad. Japan uses 100V and type A plugs. The HDMI-CEC features depend on your TV.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm one.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Any 2.1-channel Dolby Atmos soundbar with a wireless subwoofer from Sony, Samsung, LG, JBL or Sonos will do the same job. Most are larger than this one.
