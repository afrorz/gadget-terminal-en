Koizumi Seiki makes home appliances in Japan. Its new KPH-1226A/K is a ceramic heater with a panel that mimics a fireplace flame. It has no real fire. The price is open, and Koizumi's direct shop sells it for 19,800 yen, at time of writing, per Kaden Watch.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Heating | 1,200W / 750W, two steps |
| Flame panel | Artificial flame, 3 brightness levels plus off, "flicker" mode |
| Room temperature mode | Stronger air when the room is 4C or more below the set temperature, weaker when under 4C, stops at the set temperature |
| Motion sensor | Stops after about 2 minutes with no one present, restarts on movement, per Kaden Watch |
| Timers | Off timer of 1 / 2 / 4 hours per Kaden Watch; Koizumi's page says about 1 to 4 hours. Auto-off after 5 hours |
| Safety | Dual tip-over switch, child lock |
| Controls | Touch panel and a magnetic remote |
| Size | About 355 x 135 x 355mm |
| Weight | About 4.4kg |
| Power | AC 100V, 50/60Hz, 1,200W |
| Cord | About 1.6m |
| Colour | Black |

## Why it is interesting

The flame panel can run without any heat. That lets the unit work as a decorative light in warm months. In heating mode it adds a room temperature mode and a motion sensor that cuts power when the room is empty.

Koizumi's page lists a running cost of about 37.2 yen, at time of writing. We could not confirm the conditions behind that figure.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Not realistically. It is built for Japan's 100V grid and we found no overseas version.**

- **Where:** Koizumi's own online shop in Japan and Japanese retailers. We could not confirm shipping abroad.
- **The catch:** The rating is AC 100V at 1,200W. On 220 to 240V mains it would draw more than twice the current it was designed for. A step-down transformer would have to carry about 1.2kW continuously, which is a poor idea for a heater. It should not be used without a proper converter.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm one.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Electric fireplace-style heaters with a flame effect are sold in many markets by brands such as Dimplex and Duraflame. Look for models rated for your local voltage.
