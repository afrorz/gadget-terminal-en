Mantsu Shoji sells kitchen goods in Japan under the TOKAIZ name. Its "multi-function fryer" TMF-Choice1 went on sale on 20 October 2026. It is a hot-air fryer, the kind sold as an air fryer elsewhere. The cooking vessel is glass, so you can watch the food.

## Why it is interesting

Most air fryers have an opaque basket. This one has a transparent glass bowl, so you can check the browning without opening it. Two sizes come in the box. You swap them depending on the amount of food. The small bowl stores inside the large one when not in use.

The glass containers double as storage. The maker says you can let the food cool and close the bowl with the supplied lid. The plates and containers come off for washing. The box includes a recipe sheet with eight dishes, such as fried chicken, ratatouille, sweet potato and chocolate scones.

The two Japanese sources agree on the specs. The price differs: the official page lists 15,180 yen and Kaden Watch gives a direct-sale price of 13,800 yen. We did not see an independent test.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any overseas sale. Treat it as Japan-only for now.**

- **Where:** The official page links to a purchase page in Japan. We found no overseas store and no mention of shipping abroad. Japanese retailers and proxy services are the likely route, but we did not check them.
- **The catch:** The fryer is rated for AC 100V at 1,000W. That is the Japanese standard with A-type plugs. In regions with 220 to 240V mains, it will not run safely without a step-down transformer, and we cannot say a transformer would suit this load. The manual and touch-panel labels are in Japanese. We could not confirm FCC or CE marks.
- **Global version:** We found no English product page and no overseas launch announcement.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Air fryers are sold by many brands in most markets, and some have glass bowls. We did not compare them with this model.
