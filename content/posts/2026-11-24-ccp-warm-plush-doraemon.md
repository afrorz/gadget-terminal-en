CCP is a Tokyo appliance company. Its Charanics brand puts popular characters on small electronics. The newest item is a Doraemon plush with a heater inside. The product name is "Attaka Nuigurumi Doraemon", or "warm plush toy". The model number is KS-AN66-DDR.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Height | About 32cm |
| Heating | Back warms to about 42 to 46°C in about 30 seconds |
| Auto-off | After about 60 minutes |
| Power | USB Type-A, DC 5V 2A |
| Power use | 4.5W |
| Cable | Attached, about 1.8m |
| Materials | Plush: polyester. Cable: PVC |
| List price (at time of writing) | 7,260 yen with tax |
| Release | Mid-December 2026 |

## Why it is interesting

This is a desk-worker's lap warmer. CCP says you plug it into a PC, rest it on your knees and let the back warm up. It also works from a power bank, according to the company. There is no battery inside. The cable is fixed to the body.

The brand has done this before with other characters. Doraemon is one of Japan's best-known manga characters, so the plush is aimed at fans and at office workers in cold rooms. The 60-minute timer cuts power if you forget to switch it off.

CCP notes that the photos were taken during development and may differ slightly from the final product. We have not seen an independent review. All figures here come from the maker and Kaden Watch.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any overseas sale. Treat it as Japan-only for now.**

- **Where:** Pre-orders opened on 24 November on CCP's official online store in Japan. From mid-December it will go to online shops and general-goods stores in Japan, per the press release. The release does not mention overseas shipping. A Japanese proxy shopping service is the likely route, but we did not check whether any of them takes this item.
- **The catch:** It runs on 5V USB, so voltage and plugs are not a problem. You need a USB Type-A port or a power bank with one. There is no app. The packaging and manual will be in Japanese. We could not confirm FCC or CE marks, and we could not confirm whether the warranty applies abroad. The character is licensed by Japanese rights holders, so an overseas launch is not something we can assume.
- **Global version:** We found no English product page and no overseas announcement.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** USB heated plush toys and heated pads are made by several brands in many markets. We did not compare them with this one.
