Nitori is a Sapporo-based furniture and home goods chain. In mid-November 2026 it began selling the NQ898, a small hair dryer, in some Nitori stores and on Nitori's web shop. The price is 7,990 yen including tax, at time of writing. Its main selling point is travel: it takes 100 to 240V, so it needs no voltage converter.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | About 310g (without cord and hood) |
| Size | About 40 x 92 x 145mm per Kaden Watch; 4 x 9 x 15cm per the Nitori product page |
| Airflow | Up to 18m/s |
| Airflow settings | 2 (low / high) |
| Heat settings | 3 (cool / medium / high) |
| Warm air temperature | About 42.5C (medium) and 46.5C (high), measured 20cm from the outlet, per Kaden Watch |

| Rated power | 650W at 100V, 850W at 240V, per Kaden Watch |
| Cord | About 1.7m, Type A plug |
| Colour | White |
| Warranty | 1 year |
| Price | 7,990 yen, at time of writing |

## Why it is interesting

Many Japanese appliances are built for 100V only. They can fail or burn out when plugged into 230V. A dryer made for the Japanese market that explicitly accepts up to 240V is unusual, and Nitori says so in its release.

Nitori says the airflow matches its previous top model, the NL701, when both run on 100V. That is Nitori's own comparison. We have not seen an independent test.

The plug is the catch. It is a Type A plug, the two flat pins used in Japan and North America. In most of Europe, the UK and Australia you would need a travel adapter.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Not from Nitori directly, as far as we could confirm. But the dryer is made to be carried out of Japan.**

- **Where:** Some Nitori stores in Japan and Nitori's Japanese web shop, nitori-net.jp. We could not confirm whether nitori-net.jp ships this item abroad.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm a version sold outside Japan or any foreign model number.
- **The catch:** The 100 to 240V input makes it safe on foreign mains. The Type A plug needs an adapter in most regions. We could not confirm CE, UKCA or FCC marking.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Compact travel hair dryers with dual voltage and folding handles are common from Remington, Philips, Wahl and others. Check the voltage range printed on the label before you buy.
