CIO is a Japanese maker of chargers, power banks and cables. The Flex Cable 40G CtoC 7cm is a very short USB-C to USB-C cable. The cable itself is about 7cm. With the connectors, it is about 13cm end to end. Pre-orders open on 27 November 2026.

Short cables are usually charge-only or slow. This one claims the full set of features.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Length | About 7cm of cable; about 13cm including connectors |
| Data | Up to 40Gbps |
| Charging | Up to 240W (USB Power Delivery 3.1) |
| Video | Up to 8K at 60Hz via DisplayPort Alt Mode |
| Build | Flat cable with a TPE coating |
| Color | Black |
| Accessory | A separate carabiner cable holder is sold on its own |
| Price | 1,880 yen, at time of writing |

## Why it is interesting

A 13cm cable has a clear use. It joins a phone to a power bank stacked behind it, or a phone to a small external SSD, without a loop of slack. Taisy0 points to both uses.

The surprising part is the spec sheet. Cables this short are often limited to USB 2.0 speeds. CIO says this one carries 40Gbps data and 8K video. These are the maker's figures. We have not seen independent tests, and we could not confirm which USB or Thunderbolt certification it carries.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Probably, through a reseller or by shipping from Japan, but we could not confirm an official overseas sale.**

- **Where:** Taisy0 says the cable is listed on Amazon, presumably Amazon.co.jp. International shipping depends on the listing. Tokyo-based eBay sellers ship other CIO products abroad. We did not find this cable on eBay yet.
- **The catch:** A USB-C cable has no voltage or plug problem. The only risk is a counterfeit or unrated listing, so buy from a known seller. We found no FCC or CE listing for it.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm one. Nikkei Asia and ChannelNews reported earlier that CIO planned to enter the US and Australia. Those reports are old, and we do not know whether this cable is part of it.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Short USB4 or Thunderbolt 4 cables rated 40Gbps and 240W, from Anker, Cable Matters, Belkin and others, are sold worldwide. Most are 20cm or longer.
