Doushisha is an Osaka maker of household goods. Its SmartBadge is a round "digital pin badge" with a small screen. Japanese fans of idols, anime and pets use badges like this to carry a favorite photo on a bag. The new Findmine model, due in late November 2026, adds Apple's Find My network. If the bag is lost, the badge can be located on a map in the iPhone Find My app.

## Why it is interesting

The badge itself is a screen and a pin. You send photos, GIFs or short videos from your phone. The app can add stickers, effects and video trimming. It can run a slideshow. Two badges can share images wirelessly, which Doushisha compares to swapping stickers.

The Find My part is the new piece. Doushisha says registering the badge in the Find My app lets you see its location, and that it can alert you when you leave it behind. Use requires Apple devices. Doushisha adds that location checks follow the rules of Apple's network.

Memory is small. 16MB holds a limited number of photos and short clips. We did not find an independent test of screen quality, battery life or tracking accuracy. The release does not give a battery size or charging method, and we could not confirm them.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any sale outside Japan.**

- **Where:** In Japan it goes on sale in late November through Doushisha's online store, Loft stores and Loft's net store, and electronics and specialty shops. We did not find an overseas store, and we did not check whether Japanese shops ship it abroad. Proxy services such as Buyee or ZenMarket are an option we did not test.
- **The catch:** Find My needs an iPhone or other Apple device. Android phones cannot use that part. The companion app is described as free, but we could not confirm that it is available in English or in app stores outside Japan. We did not find FCC or CE listings for this Bluetooth device.
- **Global version:** We found no overseas announcement.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Small screen badges and pins for photos exist from several brands, and separate Find My trackers are sold widely. We did not find another badge that combines both.
