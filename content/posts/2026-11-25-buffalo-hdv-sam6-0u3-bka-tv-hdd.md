In Japan many people still record TV broadcasts to an external hard drive plugged into the TV. Buffalo, a Japanese PC peripheral maker, sells a line of drives made for that job. On 25 November 2026 it added a 6TB model, the HDV-SAM6.0U3-BKA, to the HDV-SAMU3-A series. The price reported by AV Watch is 78,210 yen at time of writing.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Capacity | 6TB (the series also has 4TB, 3TB and 2TB) |
| Interface | USB 3.2 Gen 1 (Micro-B), up to 5.0Gbps |
| File system | NTFS |
| Size | 114 x 33 x 171mm, horizontal placement only |
| Weight | About 900g |
| Power | AC 100V, 50/60Hz, 18W maximum |
| Cooling | Fanless |
| Warranty | 1 year |
| Price | 78,210 yen at time of writing (per AV Watch) |

## Why it is interesting

The feature is a usage meter. LEDs on the front show how full the drive is, so you can judge how much recording time is left. It does not work with every TV. Buffalo lists some models from Orion, Mitsubishi Electric and LG Electronics, SKY PerfecTV tuners and some Panasonic camcorders as unsupported.

The drive is built for long, steady use. Buffalo says it supports 24-hour continuous recording and uses a drive equivalent to the one inside Panasonic's DIGA recorders. It is also a recommended add-on for DIGA recorders and VIERA TVs. With a DIGA that supports three simultaneous recordings, the drive can take all three.

Noise is a selling point. The case is fanless, blocks vent openings that leak sound, and uses silicone rubber for vibration. A failure warning called "Mimamori Aizu for AV" lights an LED before a fault. There is a paid service where Buffalo moves recordings to a new drive.

Buffalo says a 6TB drive adds about 748 hours of terrestrial digital recording or about 530 hours of BS digital recording on a TV or recorder. These are the maker's estimates, and some TVs cannot use drives of 3TB or more.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any sale outside Japan, and the drive is built around Japanese TV systems.**

- **Where:** AV Watch links Amazon.co.jp and Rakuten for it. We did not check whether they ship it abroad, and we did not find a Buffalo store for other countries. Proxy services such as Buyee or ZenMarket are an option we did not test.
- **The catch:** The recording function is for Japanese TVs and recorders. Buffalo says playback of recordings made on a DIGA needs that DIGA. The power supply is rated for 100V. Outside Japan, a TV would not record Japanese broadcasts, so the drive would work only as an ordinary USB drive. The Buffalo page does not say whether the meter works with TVs outside Japan. We could not confirm FCC or CE marks.
- **Global version:** We found no overseas announcement.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Desktop 3.5-inch USB drives of 6TB are sold by many brands worldwide. They do not have this meter, and we did not compare specific models.
