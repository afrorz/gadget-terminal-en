Elecom is an Osaka company that sells computer and phone accessories in Japan. On 25 November 2026 it announced the Next Li+, a 5,000mAh power bank that goes on sale in December. Its pitch is lower fire risk. Power bank fires have become a common news item in Japan, and Elecom is answering with a design change rather than a new cell chemistry.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Capacity | 5,000mAh (18.5Wh) |
| Ports | USB-C and USB-A |
| Output | Up to 22.5W per port; 15W total when both are used |
| Input | USB-C, up to 18W; about 2 hours 30 minutes to charge the bank |
| Size | About 69.6 x 106.1 x 10.6mm |
| Weight | About 110g |
| Cycle life | About 1,000 charge cycles (JIS C 8711 test) |
| In the box | 0.1m USB-C cable, manual, insulation sticker |
| Colors | Black, white, ice blue, mint green, powder pink and a "Shirochan" face design |
| Price | 5,980 yen, at time of writing; 4,980 yen for limited pre-orders on the Elecom Direct Shop until 31 December |
| Warranty | 1 year |

## Why it is interesting

Elecom has sold power banks with iron-phosphate cells (2022), sodium-ion cells (2025) and semi-solid cells (2026). Kaden Watch notes that Next Li+ is not a new battery type. It is Elecom's own name for a lithium-ion cell with safety changes. Elecom lists three: a ceramic coating on the separator, an additive in the electrolyte, and a separator material that resists shrinking at high temperature.

The more unusual feature is the Health Monitor. The LED changes color and lighting pattern according to how many charge cycles the bank has been through. It is a prompt to replace the battery, not a charge gauge. Elecom also lists automatic shutdown when a fault is detected and temperature monitoring.

At 10.6mm thick and 110g, it is close to the size of a standard 5,000mAh bank. Elecom says that was the point. Independent tests of the safety claims are not available yet, so treat them as the maker's statements.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any sale outside Japan.**

- **Where:** In Japan, the Elecom Direct Shop opened limited pre-orders on 25 November. Wider retail from December is expected, but the releases do not name shops. We did not find an Elecom overseas store listing for this model, and we did not check whether Elecom Direct ships abroad.
- **The catch:** The release lists Japan's PSE mark. We could not confirm FCC or CE marks. Elecom also says the bank can be returned through JBRC, a Japanese battery collection scheme. That does not apply elsewhere.
- **Global version:** We found no overseas announcement.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** 5,000mAh banks with 20W-class output are sold by many brands in most markets. Safety-focused cells such as semi-solid and sodium-ion are rarer, and we did not compare specific products.
