Thanko is a Tokyo company known for odd, small appliances. On 25 November 2026 it released a pot-pie maker built around a monster from the Japanese TV series Kaito Sentai Lupinranger VS Patranger. The character is Salmon Shakekistantin, whose catchphrase in episode 45 is, roughly, "Eat salmon at Christmas." The price is 7,480 yen including tax, at time of writing. Thanko says it is a limited-quantity run.

## Why it is interesting

The machine itself is simple. You roll out a frozen pie sheet, cut it with the supplied round cutter, add filling, close the lid and wait. Thanko suggests salmon or ready-made stew. Because it bakes two pies at once, you can make two different fillings.

The unusual part is the licence. The body design is based on the villain, the box is modelled on his safe, and the instruction card is styled after the Red Dial Fighter. A web search summary of Japanese coverage mentions a salmon grill from the same series last year. We did not confirm that.

Without the franchise, it is a basic two-cavity pie press. The tie-in is the reason for the price and the limited run.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**Not officially, as far as we could confirm. A Japanese reseller or proxy service is the realistic route.**

- **Where:** Thanko's Japanese web shop, Japanese retailers and online stores, per the press release. We could not confirm whether Thanko ships this item abroad.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm any version sold outside Japan.
- **The catch:** It is rated for AC 100V. Do not plug it into 220 to 240V mains without a step-down transformer rated for 900W or more. We could not confirm whether it has CE, UKCA or FCC marking. The plug type is not stated in our sources, though Japanese 100V appliances normally use a Type A plug.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Sandwich and pie makers with removable plates are common in Europe, the UK and North America. Check the voltage label for your country.
