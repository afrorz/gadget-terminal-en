Xiaomi has opened crowdfunding on Xiaomi Youpin, its China-only shopping platform, for a tiny Bluetooth speaker that sticks to the back of your phone with magnets. The crowdfunding price is 132 yuan, and the regular price after launch will be 199 yuan (prices at time of writing). It weighs about 95g and doubles as a phone stand.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Weight | about 95g |
| Driver | 1.5-inch 3W full-range, RGB ring light |
| Battery | 650mAh |
| Water/dust | IP67 |
| Connection | Bluetooth, stereo pairing, chain up to 10 units |
| Price (China) | 132 yuan crowdfunding / 199 yuan retail |

## It powers on when it touches your phone

A magnetic ring and a Hall sensor sit in the base. Snap it onto a MagSafe iPhone (iPhone 12 or later) and it switches on. Pull it off and it goes to sleep. You don't have to reconnect Bluetooth each time. Android users get a magnetic ring in the box to stick on the back of their phone. It also sticks to fridges and metal pegboards.

This is still a crowdfunding campaign, not a shipping product. Xiaomi is a large company, so the risk of it never shipping is lower than with a typical Kickstarter project. But the funding goal and backer count were not published in the reports we checked.

## Can you get it outside China?

**Not directly, for now.** Xiaomi Youpin sells to mainland China and does not normally ship abroad. To get one today you would need a buying agent (a service that orders from Chinese stores and forwards the package to you).

- **Global version:** Xiaomi has not announced one. Many Youpin crowdfunding products later appear on AliExpress or in Xiaomi's global stores, but that is not confirmed for this speaker.
- **Certification:** We could not confirm FCC (US) or CE (EU) certification. Youpin products are usually made for the Chinese market first.
- **What to do:** If you want one now, use a buying agent and accept that there is no international warranty. If you can wait, watch for a global launch. We will update this article if one is announced.
