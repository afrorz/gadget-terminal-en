Captain Stag is a camping brand from Sanjo in Niigata. Its camera accessory line, CSP (Captain Stag Photo), has added a plain item: a wooden box for a camera you use every day. Pre-orders opened on 20 November 2026 at Yodobashi.com. Sales start on 4 December.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Model | UM-1672 |
| Material | Natural acacia wood, finished with vegetable oil |
| Outer size | About 365 × 275 × 160mm (W × D × H) |
| Inner size | About 325 × 235 × 135mm |
| Weight | About 3kg |
| Where sold | Yodobashi.com at launch |

We did not find a price in the press release. We could not confirm one.

## Why it is interesting

A dry cabinet protects a camera from damp. It is also a chore. Captain Stag's argument is that a camera you put away every time gets used less. The box is meant for the one or two cameras you reach for daily, kept on a shelf or desk. Lenses and cameras you rarely use should still go in a dry cabinet. The company says the box does not replace one.

It has no electronics, so nothing is tied to a Japanese app, voltage or radio rule. The appeal is the material. Each box has a different grain, because the wood is natural, and the oil finish keeps the surface close to bare wood.

CSP works with the YouTuber Yu-tobi as brand adviser, and with MGNET, a design company in the Tsubame-Sanjo area. A video from Yu-tobi, published on 20 November, introduces the product. It is mostly about camera storage, not a full review, so we do not embed it.

The campaign also gives 15 percent point back on all 23 CSP items at Yodobashi until 11 January 2027. That only applies to Japanese point accounts.

## Can you get it outside Japan?

**We could not confirm any overseas sale. Treat it as Japan-only for now.**

- **Where:** The press release names only Yodobashi.com. Yodobashi has an international page that says overseas buyers should email the shop to start an order. We could not confirm that CSP items are covered, or that this box is.
- **The catch:** At about 3kg, shipping a wooden box abroad will cost more than a small accessory. We did not find a quote. Customs rules on wood products differ by country, and we did not check them.
- **Global version:** We found no English page for CSP and no sign of an overseas launch.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Wooden storage and display boxes are sold by many makers. We did not compare sizes for camera bodies.
