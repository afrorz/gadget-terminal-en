Xiaomi has added a Sand Gold colour to its Mijia Multifunctional Portable Flashlight. According to Gizmochina, the price stays at 99 yuan at time of writing. The light itself is not new. IT之家 reported its launch in an earlier year, with the same 99 yuan price.

We could not read the Gizmochina page or Xiaomi's store page. We confirmed the Sand Gold colour only through Gizmochina's summary. The specs below come from IT之家's report on the original model, and Gizmochina's summary agrees on price and brightness. We could not confirm that the Sand Gold version has identical specs.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Brightness | 1,000 lumens |
| Range | More than 150m |
| Battery | 3,100mAh lithium, up to about 30 hours of runtime (IT之家) |
| Modes | High beam, ambient light, candle light, emergency light |
| Rating | IP65 dust and water resistance |
| Size / weight | About 3cm across, about 125g |
| Body | Aluminium, according to Gizmochina |
| Price | 99 yuan in China (at time of writing) |

## Why it is interesting

It is a plain tool. It is small, rechargeable and has a wide beam range for the money. Xiaomi sells it as a household and glovebox light, and the new colour changes only the look.

IT之家 later noted JD.com deal prices well under the list price for the black model. We did not check whether the Sand Gold one has the same discounts.

## Can you get it outside China?

**Yes for the original colours, through importers. We could not confirm the Sand Gold version.**

- **Where:** Search results show the Mijia 1,000-lumen flashlight on eBay, Galaxus and Digitec in Europe, and on smaller import shops. These listings show the original colours, not Sand Gold. We did not confirm current prices or stock.
- **The catch:** These are third-party sellers, not Xiaomi. Several listings describe different Mijia flashlight models, so check lumens, battery and the IP rating before you buy.
- **Global version:** We could not confirm a Xiaomi global announcement for this flashlight or for the Sand Gold colour.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** Rechargeable 1,000-lumen flashlights from Anker, Olight and Fenix are sold through the usual stores in most countries.
