AOC has put a new portable monitor on sale in China. The Q16T36 has a 16-inch 2.5K IPS panel, a 120Hz refresh rate and two USB-C ports. It is listed on JD.com for 999 yuan at time of writing.

We could not read AOC's own product page or the JD.com listing. The specs below come from Notebookcheck and GuruHiTech, and the numbers match between them unless noted. One number does not match across reports: the resolution. Notebookcheck lists 2560×1600, while the Chinese report from IT之家 lists QHD 2560×1440.

## Specs

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Panel | 16-inch IPS, 8-bit |
| Resolution | 2.5K (2560×1600 per Notebookcheck, 2560×1440 per IT之家) |
| Refresh rate | 120Hz (GuruHiTech adds 4ms GtG response) |
| Brightness | 350 nits |
| Contrast | 1,200:1 |
| Colour | 99% sRGB, Delta E under 2 |
| Ports | 2× USB-C, HDMI 2.0 (GuruHiTech also lists a 3.5mm jack) |
| Charging | Up to 45W to a connected device, only when the monitor itself runs on mains power |
| Speakers | 1W |
| Mounting | Built-in stand, 75×75mm VESA |
| Price | 999 yuan on JD.com (at time of writing) |

## Why it is interesting

Most cheap portable monitors stay at 1080p and 60Hz. AOC's own 16-inch models sold outside China, such as the 16T3E and 16T3EA, are 1080p and 60Hz. The Q16T36 moves to a sharper 2.5K panel with a faster refresh rate at a price that is low for the specs.

The 45W charging is a useful extra with a catch. It works as a pass-through for a laptop or phone, but the monitor needs its own power supply first. Over HDMI, the monitor also needs separate USB-C power.

The 350 nits of brightness is modest. This is a screen for indoor use.

## Can you get it outside China?

**Not from AOC at the moment, and we found no importer listing.**

- **Where:** The only listing we found is on JD.com in China. We did not find it on AliExpress, Amazon or a Western AOC store.
- **The catch:** Notebookcheck says there is no word on international availability. GuruHiTech says no sale in Italy or Europe has been confirmed. Warranty on a JD.com purchase shipped abroad is something we could not confirm.
- **Global version:** AOC has not announced one. We could not confirm whether it will use the Q16T36 name outside China.
- **Alternatives you can buy locally:** The Ugreen AP16 is a 16-inch 2.5K 165Hz portable monitor that Notebookcheck reported has a US release. The ASUS ZenScreen MB16QHG is a 16-inch 2560×1600 120Hz portable monitor listed by European retailers.
